Build a clean watchlist from Amplify ETF constituents.

This static browser app follows the single-file approach from daggerok/iShares, but uses Amplify's public ETF catalog and Firestore-backed holdings data. Choose the funds you care about, filter out cash, Treasuries, money-market rows, and option strings by preset, then export symbols in CSV or TXT format.

How to use

1. Search or filter the ETF catalog and select one or more funds.

2. Click Load selected holdings to pull the latest holdings document for each fund.

3. Adjust the symbol preset, then copy a deduped watchlist or export the full table.

ETF catalog

Live categories are read from Amplify's public Firestore data; an embedded Our ETFs fallback keeps the app usable offline.

Watchlist controls

Selections persist locally in this browser. Holdings are fetched on demand.

ℹ️
Default filtering removes cash, money-market funds, Treasury/CUSIP-like identifiers, option contracts, and derivative/futures strings. Use Raw holdings when you want to audit every line item exactly as Amplify publishes it.
